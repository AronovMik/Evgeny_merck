## Результаты
Все значения — из блока `Summary`, [абстракт]; номеров страниц оригинала в файле нет.

**Фармакодинамика** (раздел «Pharmacodynamic Studies»):
- «Animal studies have shown that bisoprolol is a more potent antagonist of β1-adrenoceptors than atenolol or metoprolol, but the drug appears to be less potent than propranolol and betaxolol in this regard.» — качественно, числовых значений потенции в файле нет.
- Снижение нагрузочной тахикардии: «significant reductions in exercise tachycardia (about 20%)… 24 hours after oral administration of 5 and 10mg» — у пациентов со стабильной стенокардией.
- АД: «Both systolic and diastolic blood pressures are reduced by bisoprolol (by up to about 20%, respectively, in healthy subjects and in patients with mild to moderate essential hypertension)».
- Потребность миокарда в кислороде: «as well as myocardial oxygen demand (by up to 34%)».
- Дыхание, здоровые: «Respiratory function in healthy subjects was not affected by bisoprolol 20 and 40mg orally».
- Дыхание, астматики: «oral bisoprolol 10 and 20mg and metoprolol 100mg significantly reduced peak expiratory flow rate (p<0.01 vs placebo), while bisoprolol 10mg also significantly decreased vital capacity and forced expiratory volume in 1 second (p<0.01 vs placebo). The patients remained asymptomatic and all of these changes in ventilatory parameters were rapidly reversed by inhaled terbutaline.»
- Углеводный обмен: «In non-diabetic healthy subjects bisoprolol did not significantly affect insulin-induced hypoglycaemia or the compensatory rise in serum lactate concentration after insulin. In hypertensive non-insulin-dependent diabetics bisoprolol had no effect on carbohydrate metabolism.»
- Липиды: «Statistically significant increases in serum triglycerides (p<0.05) and reductions in high density lipoprotein (HDL)-cholesterol (bisoprolol, p<0.01; atenolol, p<0.05) were reported in a 3-month comparison of oral bisoprolol 10 and 20 mg/day and atenolol 50 and 100 mg/day in patients with mild to moderate essential hypertension.» Абсолютные значения по группам: `НЕ НАЙДЕНО В ФАЙЛЕ`. Это **только внутригрупповая динамика** — p-значения приведены отдельно для бисопролола и отдельно для атенолола; **межгруппового сравнения в файле нет**.

**Фармакокинетика** (раздел «Pharmacokinetic Studies»):
- Биодоступность: «bioavailability ⩾ 90% after oral administration» (в Synopsis — «The oral bioavailability of bisoprolol is high (90%)»).
- Tmax: «Peak plasma concentrations are reached within 3 hours of a single 10mg dose in healthy subjects».
- Cmax: «maximum plasma concentrations of between 36 and 78 μg/L… after the same dose in healthy subjects and patients with varying degrees of renal and hepatic insufficiency».
- Распределение (животные): «Animal studies indicate that bisoprolol is rapidly and widely distributed, but little placental transfer occurs and the drug only penetrates the blood-brain barrier to a small degree in comparison to metoprolol and propranolol.»
- Связывание с белками: «Bisoprolol binds to human plasma proteins to the extent of about 30%.»
- Элиминация: «Approximately 50% of a dose of bisoprolol is hepatically metabolised to 3 inactive metabolites while the rest is renally excreted unchanged; in addition, ⩽ 10% of the drug undergoes ‘first-pass’ metabolism.» В Synopsis: «it is hepatically and renally cleared in equal proportions».
- T1/2: «The elimination half-life of unchanged bisoprolol in healthy subjects is 9 to 12 hours and is increased to about 18 hours in patients with renal impairment or to about 13 hours in patients with hepatic cirrhosis.»

**Терапевтические исследования** (раздел «Therapeutic Trials»):
- АГ, оптимальный диапазон доз: «5 to 20mg once daily».
- АГ, снижение АД: «Systolic and diastolic blood pressures were well controlled with a single daily dose of bisoprolol (15 to 20% reductions from baseline)».
- АГ, достижение цели: «in long term non-comparative studies reductions in diastolic blood pressure to ⩽ 90mm Hg were achieved in 70 to 95% of patients».
- АГ, сравнения: «bisoprolol 5 to 20mg once daily was as effective as atenolol 50 to 100 mg/day, metoprolol 100 mg/day and nifedipine SR 40 to 80 mg/day, and more effective than daily treatment with hydrochlorothiazide 50mg plus amiloride 5mg». Числовых эффектов, ДИ, p — нет.
- Стенокардия, 12 мес: «approximately 50% of the patients were completely free from anginal attacks, with 27%, 55% and 18% of the patients receiving bisoprolol 5mg, 10mg and 20mg daily, respectively».
- Стенокардия, сравнения: «bisoprolol 5 to 10mg once daily and atenolol 100mg once daily produced similar increases in exercise duration, time to onset of exercise-induced ischaemia and reductions in the frequency of anginal attacks, glyceryl trinitrate (nitroglycerin) consumption and myocardial oxygen demand»; «in combination therapy with isosorbide dinitrate 20mg twice daily, bisoprolol 10 to 20mg once daily or verapamil 240 to 360 mg/day produced comparable improvements in exercise duration, myocardial ischaemia and oxygen demand, frequency of anginal attacks and glyceryl trinitrate consumption».

## Ограничения по мнению авторов
Формального раздела «Limitations» нет; авторы формулируют оговорки внутри синопсиса:
1. Окончательная оценка места бисопролола в терапии АГ и стенокардии невозможна до получения результатов дальнейших сравнительных исследований с established agents («the results of further comparative studies with established agents… are still awaited so that a final assessment of the relative place in therapy of bisoprolol… may be made», Synopsis).
2. Общее влияние бисопролола на липидный обмен остаётся неясным, требуются дальнейшие исследования («the overall effects of bisoprolol on lipid metabolism are as yet still unclear and further studies in this area are required», Pharmacodynamic Studies).
3. Сравнительные исследования при стенокардии включали лишь малое число пациентов, необходимы более крупные («these comparative studies involved only small numbers of patients, and further comparative studies in larger patient groups are required to confirm their findings», Therapeutic Trials).
4. Преимущества уникальных фармакокинетических свойств сформулированы предположительно («its unique pharmacokinetic properties **may** offer advantages in selected patients», Synopsis).
